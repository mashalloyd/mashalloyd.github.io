"Burnham determined to take UK back into the EU", the sun came out and we took Pippa on her first walk since her recovery, the three world leaders meet in China, Sánchez loses general election debate on TV, a family lunch and other stories of the week.

Madrid, Sunday 22nd November, 2026

 Madrid, Sunday 22nd November, 2026

With Elliot yesterday doing a world map jigsaw puzzle
Good morning everyone, this, for once, sunny and dry Sunday at the end of November. Christmas is nearly in a month's time but I haven't started on any preparations yet. 

We have not been on any travels since our return at the end of October from our mini road trip to Morocco, thus again I am writing from home. I am longing for better weather and for a trip somewhere which doesn't even have to be exotic. Don't get me wrong; I love our home but also love travelling. 

It is three weeks since Pippa became seriously ill and was diagnosed with Hypothyroid Polyneuropathy which left her nearly totally paralysed. Well, this week I am happy to announce even more progress. Her blood test came back fine with the right levels of T4 and TSH which means the meds are doing their work and the dose was correct. This week saw her resume her walks and I even saw her running a few times. We have got her back and she has quality of life. Next week she will turn 12 which is ancient for a dog but I am hoping that with the meds and our loving care, she will live for quite a few years more.

As always in my weekly posts, let me start from last Sunday. It rained all day, Tana was away and all our guests had gone apart from Mark so we were alone really until Suzy joined us for lunch. She has such a busy social life and work schedule these days that we hardly see her. I made roast lamb with all the trimmings which was delicious. I was tempted to make Yorkshire pudding too but I didn't.
Sunday roast with shoulder of lamb for lunch last week
We had a quite afternoon until we got an emergency call from Olivia. She had cut her finger while chopping vegetables for a soup and demanded our presence immediately. We live very near but just getting our shoes on and the car out takes some time. By the tone of her voice, I imagined she had chopped her finger completely. But she hadn't. It didn't even need stitches. While she and Miguel were at A&E, we babysat for the kids and they behaved beautifully. Elliot wanted me to play chess with him which I haven't played since at St. Joseph's College, nearly 60 years ago. It was the history teacher, Mr. Lenton who taught us. I had to get Elliot to remind me of some of the moves and rules. I was delighted to notice how when moving a piece he  thinks ahead. I did manage to take a few of his pieces and we were equal when his parents got back. I imagine very soon he will be a good player and I will remember playing with him with fondness.

We were home on time for a miniscule dinner after which we were soon tucked up in bed with Pippa and watching the news. For the first time in ages, we watched something on Netflix. It was a Spanish series called Murder in Galicia which we both enjoyed. 

Monday came and Eladio was up early for a blood test. He just did it for routine purposes. It rained again and we didn't leave the house. I have to say I spent the day doing practically nothing. But I did read the papers on Internet of course. I am happy to quote the new British Labour PM, Andy Burnham who is tackling the elephant in the room finally, the latter being Brexit. It was music to my ears to hear him say: "It's like the Emperor's new clothes - where no one is prepared to say the obvious truth. Well, I say it, as I see it. And to be frank, the emperor has nothing on". Yesterday morning the Times Headlines was even more music to my ears. It ran like this: "Burnham determined to take UK back into the EU". He made a start on Friday when he attended the UK EU Summit in Brussels. Hurray is all I can say. 

Of note, on the other side of the world, Pope Leo XIV, who was on a visit to his beloved Peru that day, was hit on the face by someone throwing a rosary! Whoever did it must have felt mortified.

Tuesday dawned and it was lambs to the slaughter for me. That morning I was having a cystoscopy; a camera up the urethra and bladder. Honestly it was awful and I actually cried while it happened even though I was given some sort of sedative gel. It was part of a battery of tests for my recurrent UTI which is such a bore. However, I liked the woman urologist who did the awful procedure and she offered to take on my case and I am seeing her tomorrow. Also tomorrow I am seeing the urogynaecologist, the woman doctor who did my prolapse operation a year ago. I would have gone to her from the very beginning but she had changed jobs. Finally, though I found out where to and it's at a fancy new private hospital very near here. If only I had known before. Meanwhile the problem persists and I am still on antibiotics I am sick of and aren't doing their job. So watch this space.

The news that day was that the new James Bond will be an actor called Robert Pattinson whom I have never heard of. There can never be a better James Bond than Sean Connery in my mind but let's give the guy a chance. I am glad they didn't chose a black man or a woman which may have been on the cards. I bet Ian Fleming is glad too. The other news was that 5 EU countries have agreed to deport illegal immigrants not given asylum to bloody Rwanda. They are Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and Holland. The Brits already tried that idea and it didn't work. I don't think it will work this time either but may put off so many illegal immigrants trying to reach the EU. This week in Spain the immigration crisis continued as migrants arrived en masse to the Canary Islands, mainly to the small island of El Hierro. 
A "cayuco" boat arriving at the Island of El Hierro this week, just one of hundreds

Immigration is one of the key topics in the political campaign leading up to the general elections next Sunday 29th November. The general elections  seem to be nearly the only news on TV here and they are being heavily contested. I am a democrat and hate extremes; fascism and communism but I also want a decent and non corrupt government so want to see Sánchez ousted. The only alternative is Alberto Núñez Feijóo, the leader of the PP right wing party. He is likely to win but to govern will need a coalition with the far right party, Vox. That can only be better than what we have now. 

On Wednesday I spent part of the morning writing up my urological medical history which goes back to when I was a child. It is for the new urologist. AI did a great job tweaking it. I multi tasked and made far too much cream of red pepper soup which I have had to freeze. We went out finally that morning and I had coffee at Alverán after which we did the weekly food shopping, mostly at Carrefour. I have all their loyalty cards and was happy to use a 176 euro voucher to do my shopping this week. 

On Thursday finally the sun came out. I proposed taking Pippa on her first walk since her recovery. It was cold so we put her red jumper on. Instead of going on our usual walk, we drove to Boadilla and went for a walk in the Infante Don Luis Palace gardens, a lovely place. We did so after my morning coffee at a new cafe for me; La Taberna. 
Coffee at La Taberna in Boadilla on Thursday before our walk.

We walked more slowly than usual to gauge how Pippa was doing and she was doing fine. Here are some photos of that walk with her.

With little Pippa on her first walk after her recovery

That day the three current world leaders, Vladimir Putin of Russia, Donald Trump of the US and host Xi Jinping, met in China. To think these three tyrants, these three men with the nuclear button at hand, are today's world leaders, makes me cringe. 

These three dictators, the so called 3 world leaders, met in China this week
It was the first time the three sat down together and some media likened the event to the Yalta meeting with the "Big three" in 1945. They were Stalin, Roosevelt and Churchill. Note that in this week's Big Three meeting,  there is no representative from neither the UK nor the EU. Churchill would turn in his grave.They represent the countries with most nuclear weapons in the world and Trump and Putin are war mongers. Trump is at war with Iran and Putin is at war with Ukraine. They issued a 4 point peace plan which I don't believe in. It is very vague and there is no mention of the ending of the two main wars. If only there were three women world leaders then I believe we would not have wars.

In the afternoon, a new direct guest arrived called Maria who is from Seville. She had to drive here rather than take the high speed train while the track is being repaired after the awful accident in Toledo last month. I went out to greet her and Pippa joined me; a first since her illness. I even saw her running up and down the drive, barking at the neighbours' dogs. Maria came through another direct guest, Laura and both of them are doing a masters degree in Pediatric physiotherapy. She is coming back next month so  must have liked her stay. 

We were up very late that night after watching the 1.5h long general election debate on the television. It was between the current PM, Sánchez and his right wing rival, Feijóo. 
Spain's TV "face to face" debate this week
This was a repeat of the 2023 TV debate which Feijóo won, like he did this week. However, in 2023, he did win the elections but Sánchez gathered enough seats to govern with his weak coalition government, made up of parties that want to split from Spain,  a party which grew from the ETA terrorist group and the communist party, Sumar. No wonder he hasn't been able to govern nor present a budget. Feijóo who leads in the polls, had it easy  really as there is so much to blame Sánchez for; corruption in his party, family court cases including his wife and brother, his inaction when 100,000 immigrants invaded Ceuta and of course a severe housing crisis, rising living costs as well as a failure to invest in maintaining infrastructure which is why we have had two terrible high speed train accidents since January. That morning, the Catalan fugitive,Puigdemont, who was Sanchez' king maker, was interviewed in a national newspaper. The headline could not have come on a worse day for the Spaniard. Puigdemont said: "I took advantage of Pedro Sanchez' unbridled ambition for power to push through the amnesty law and destroy Spain". The amnesty law was to pardon him for calling an illegal referendum for Independence in Catalonia. We watched the whole of the interview and I have never seen the President so unable to respond to his rival's attacks.He had run out of lies. Let's hope Feijóo not only beats Sánchez on television but in the polls next week too.

Prime time in Spain, from 10pm to midnight, is probably the latest in Europe, so the debate had us up till nearly 1 in the morning. 

Friday came and Eladio had to be up early to take the kids to school. When he was back we went on our usual walk for the first time in ages and little Pippa joined us. She was a bit slower so we didn't do the whole of it but she did very well. 

It was on Friday that Andy Burnham attended the UK-EU Summit 2026. He met with the European Commission President, Urusala von der Lyen and European Council President, Antonio Costa.
Andy Burnham in Brussels for a UK EU summit meeting on Friday
The meeting wasn't to rejoin the EU (I wish it was) but to reset the post (hard) Brexit deal. On the table were food exports, emissions, trading, security cooperation and youth movement. Unfortunately they didn't talk about general freedom of movement. As I wrote earlier, the main headline of The Times today was of Burnham being determined to take UK back into the EU. I sincerely hop he does and Friday was a start.

Yesterday was Saturday and it rained again, robbing us of a walk. Instead we went to do some errands. One of them was to pick up my mother's engagement ring as one of the turquoise stones had fallen out. I got Suzy to take a photo of it on my hand to show you. It was bought second hand in Cambridge and I have always loved it and when I wear it, which is nearly always, I remember my mother and her beautiful hands with long tapered fingers. I wish I had her hands.
I got my mother's engagement ring back from the jewelers yesterday  and am happy to have it on my hand again.
I came home to rustle up lunch for us all. Olivia was coming with the kids and Suzy would be joining us too. How wonderful. I had so much food we were spoiled for choice and everyone had something different from what I had to offer. This included: red pepper soup, chickpea soup, steak, pork cutlets, a variety of vegetables as well as mashed potatoes. This time I had to have a photo of us around the table, minus me, of course.
Family lunch yesterday
Suzy had removed her hair extensions as they itched so much. The kids behaved well and after we had cleared everything away as Tana was away, Eladio and I did a jigsaw puzzle with Elliot. It was of a world map and we had fun. The girls took a photo and I have chosen it as this week's feature photo. We all love maps and have lots on our kitchen wall. My father did too and I remember all the maps in his study at 6 Heaton Grove, including a whole wall with a map of Europe in Russia. That got me thinking maybe Elliot would like a giant world map for his room for Christmas. Oli agrees and I will be getting one soon, on Amazon, of course. 

The rest of our day was very quiet as I expect today, Sunday, will be too. It is sunny again so we shall be going on our walk with Pippa. 

Now that's it from me for this week. Sorry I don't have more exciting news but some times, no news is good news, to quote my dear late Father

Cheers till next Sunday. All the best, Masha.
